Channel Stats Reader

Channel Stats Reader is a personal tool. It reads the statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels (views, audience retention, traffic sources) so the owner can review how each video performed.

It is used only by the channel owner. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

What it accesses

  • Read-only access to the owner's YouTube channel information.
  • Read-only access to the owner's YouTube Analytics reports.

It cannot upload, edit, or delete anything.
